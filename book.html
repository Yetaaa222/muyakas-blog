About the Book

The Work No One Claps For

Before the international career, the credentials and the titles, there was a girl in torn shoes in Lusaka learning that what people see is built in seasons they never watch. UNSEEN is Muyaka Ngulube-Banda’s story: the marriage nobody knew was breaking, the body that said what her mouth would not, the pastor who taught her about seasons, and the four steps that turned twenty years of invisible work into a life she no longer has to prove.

The Full Story

Behind Every
Overnight Success

The world saw the finance professional who moved from Zambia to South Sudan, Nigeria, Indonesia and Liberia, turning around projects everyone else had given up on. The world did not see the marriage quietly breaking behind the competence. It did not see the headaches that no scan could explain, or the woman showing up every morning to a life she did not want to live.

UNSEEN is Muyaka Ngulube-Banda’s account of the seasons that made her: the girl mocked for plastic shoes, the wife carrying silence in a house that looked complete, the mother burying a son she raised, the executive who landed in a locked quarantine room and a hospital bed before she finally stopped.

Then a pastor taught her one word that reorganized everything: seasons. That nothing she was living was permanent, that every season releases something, and that what previous seasons deposit, you carry forward. In this book she shares the four steps she has lived ever since: identify your season, receive what it is releasing, carry what was deposited, and look forward to what is coming.

Raw, warm and unpretentious, UNSEEN is for anyone whose growth is happening where nobody is looking. If you are in a season nobody is watching, this book will help you understand what it is building.

A Message From Muyaka

I have been writing to you for years.

If you have followed my posts, you know I talk about promises, principles and prophecies. What God says about you. The process that releases it. What is coming.

This book is where those posts were born.

I did not write UNSEEN because my life was neat. I wrote it because for twenty years I lived through seasons nobody could see: a marriage that looked complete and was quietly breaking, a body that started saying what my mouth refused to say, a career that kept moving while I was disappearing inside it.

Then one word from a pastor reorganized my whole life: seasons.

If you are carrying something in a place nobody is watching, a business you are building, a calling you are incubating, a pain you cannot explain to anyone, I wrote this book for you. There is no access without process. Your season is not a verdict. It is a setup.

Read it slowly. Keep your pen near.

Muyaka
